Capital One Offers Miles Sorter

Sort offer tiles by highest miles, lowest miles, highest X multiples, or the original page order.

How to use it

  1. Open a page on capitaloneoffers.com.
  2. Find the Miles sorter bar above the main offers grid.
  3. Choose Highest miles, Lowest miles, Highest X multiples, or Original.

If the page shows a full-screen table or a yellow Show list button, another sorter extension or userscript is active. Disable that one first.
